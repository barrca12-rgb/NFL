How to read this

Model cover %How often the pick covers in 20,000 simulations if the model's projection is exactly right. Used to pick and rank plays; it runs high (a 70% here has historically won about 56%).
Realistic cover %The same simulation centered between the market consensus and the model (45% toward the model in Weeks 1-4, 20% after), which matches the model's real track record. It is then priced at each book's own number, so a better number at one book shows up as real value. Use this for whether a bet is worth it and how much.
EV / unitExpected profit per unit staked at the posted odds, from the realistic cover %. Above 0 means the price is worth taking.
Good down toThe worst number you can still take the bet at, at the same price. If the line moves past it, pass.
Every bookEach sportsbook's number and price for the pick. The bold one is the best; always bet there.
