How to read this
Model cover %How often the pick covers in 20,000 simulations if the model's projection is exactly right. Used to pick and rank plays; it runs high (a 70% here has historically won about 56%).
Realistic cover %The same simulation centered between the market consensus and the model (45% toward the model in Weeks 1-4, 20% after), which matches the model's real track record, then priced at each book's own number. Use this for whether a bet is worth it.
EV / unitExpected profit per unit staked at the posted odds, from the realistic cover %. Above 0 means the price is worth taking.
